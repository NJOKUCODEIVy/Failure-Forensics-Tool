CONSULTING AGREEMENT

This Consulting Agreement is made between Ferro & Vance Engineering ("Company") and Priya Raman ("Consultant").

1. Engagement. Consultant will review the structural load calculations for the Company's Eastgate warehouse expansion and deliver a written assessment.

2. Term. This Agreement starts upon signature and continues until the assessment is delivered, or until terminated by either party.

3. Compensation. Company will pay Consultant $185 per hour, not to exceed $9,000 without prior written approval. Consultant will invoice monthly.

4. Independent Contractor. Consultant is an independent contractor and not an employee of the Company.

5. Termination. Either party may terminate this Agreement upon written notice.

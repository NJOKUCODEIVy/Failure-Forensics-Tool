MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of March 1, 2026 ("Effective Date") by and between Brightline Analytics LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("Provider"), and Harbor & Pine Retail Inc. ("Client").

1. Services. Provider will deliver monthly demand-forecasting reports and maintain Client's forecasting dashboard.

2. Term. This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until February 28, 2027, unless terminated earlier under Section 5.

3. Fees. Client will pay Provider a fixed fee of $12,500.00 USD per month. Invoices are due within 30 days of receipt.

4. Confidentiality. Each party will protect the other party's confidential information using at least reasonable care.

5. Termination. Either party may terminate this Agreement with 60 days' written notice.

Signed:
Dana Okafor, CEO, Brightline Analytics LLC
Marcus Feld, COO, Harbor & Pine Retail Inc.

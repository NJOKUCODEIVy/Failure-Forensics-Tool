QUARTERLY RIDERSHIP REPORT - Q3 2026

Executive Summary
Total ridership for Q3 2026 reached 4.82 million boardings, up 6.1% from Q3
2025. Growth was concentrated on the Route 12 and Route 40 corridors follow-
ing the June schedule redesign. Weekend ridership grew faster than week-
day ridership for the second consecutive quarter.

Key Metrics
On-time performance: 87.4% (target: 90%)
Farebox recovery ratio: 23.8%
Average weekday boardings: 54,300

Recommendations
1. Extend Route 40 evening service to 11 PM to match demand.
2. Prioritize signal upgrades on the Harbor Blvd corridor, where most late
arrivals originate.
3. Revisit the 90% on-time target after the Q4 winter schedule.
